# Western backlog, shard 0 – round 4

All **65 assigned names were attempted**. Four frozen batches contain **63 valid source-based estimates**, including **29 explicitly partial footprints**. **Árkosháza-puszta and Sandipuszta remain unresolved** with concrete searched sources and reasons in `unresolved.json`; their existing fallbacks must remain. Stable exports are under `batches/`; `features.geojson` is the cumulative collection.

Batch 01 contains 15 surface/component estimates. Batch 02 adds 20 whole street-grid estimates, including housing estates, hillside quarters, traditional settlement clusters and Lánkapuszta. Krisna-völgy is explicitly a partial occupied community core, because its wider farm-property perimeter is not established. Batch 03 adds 11 individually inspected coherent field/hillside circuits plus distinct Balatonlelle quarters and the broader Tapolca eastern quarter. Batch 04 adds 13 industrial, settlement, street and field estimates; it explicitly documents use of the native municipality edge as an inferred western closure for the Katona-rét tract.

Every feature is explicitly estimated and low confidence. No circle, point-only box, Voronoi partition or source-less shape is used. Original native surfaces are preserved directly or combined through documented source-object recipes. Street envelopes use actual named street circuits and a documented inferred frontage/verge allowance. A source landuse/park/vineyard/woodland or component-group footprint is marked `partialFootprint: true` when the full locality/planning territory is not established.

Physical field circuits are polygonized from original roads, tracks, streams and parcel edges. Each accepted tract was inspected in its rendered source context, and its boundary source objects are archived. A place label associates the resulting already bounded tract with the name; it does not construct its shape. Their full historical named-field perimeters remain unknown, so these are partial, low-confidence tract estimates. Unrelated tiny urban cells, three identical Balatonlelle candidates, a woodland-only Óbrennberg candidate and a spurious long motorway-edge Diós circuit were rejected in favor of meaningful whole-quarter/street/parcel interpretations. `field-circuit-proposals.geojson` preserves those proposals as **uncollected review evidence**, not final features.

Native landscape surfaces may cross town limits. Original source geometry is retained: Sághegy is 72.0% inside Celldömölk, Nagyasszonyi-erdő woodland is 46.0% inside Balatonszárszó. They are explicitly partial landscape-footprint estimates; a town-bounded integration may clip them to its municipality. Smaller native spill is recorded for Páskom (0.6%), Huszászi-part (2.8%) and Macska-liki (0.03%). Other street estimates are clipped to their municipality. `validation.json` records all containment fractions and known-label offsets, rather than silently altering these source footprints.

Batch 01 includes three separate historical Ajka planning groups. The municipal 2008 IVS explicitly groups the component quarters. Their archived component occupied-area estimates are combined, with gaps preserved and unresolved rural/other components explicitly recorded. The grouped names are not aliases for their children and no unseen PDF map contour is claimed. ZalaZONE uses original named asphalt/module streets and operator descriptions. Malomsziget is an original explicitly named grassland polygon. Other native residential, farmyard, park, vineyard and woodland surfaces provide clearly qualified occupied/local landscape footprints.

## Offline build

```sh
PYTHONPATH=/workspace/ingatlan/.local/geodata python data/neighborhoods/expansion-20261115-round4/shard-0/build.py
```

The build requires Python, shapely and pyproj, and reads only the archived compressed `source-objects.geojson.gz` and explicit `recipes.json`. It generates cumulative and batch GeoJSON and `build-proof.json`. It does not need network, the full PBF or original PDFs. Raw selected source geometry, object IDs, tags, URLs and prior-source attribution are preserved. Reused Ajka inputs were copied from the current baseline registry together with their full provenance. New source objects come from the shared round-4 native context extraction, OSM snapshot 2026-11-12, PBF SHA256 `224897876ea131bcf0cb420e789640402d63aabe5e1491785aa4fca813aa641a`.

The final normal build and `validate.py` are fully self-contained. Optional acquisition/review scripts refer to the separately archived shared round-4 contexts; they are not required for reconstruction. `update-reports.py` regenerates the source-label/municipality review report from the archived assignment and `municipalities.geojson.gz`. The final exported features and all four batch hashes rebuild deterministically.

`evidence/` archives the web connector responses actually retrieved, including indexed primary text and opened document text. These are not claims to possess original PDF bytes or to have traced an unseen image. `context-review.json` records the source-surface and street candidates actually checked for all point-located targets. The extraction viewport is never a neighborhood boundary. Original OSM-derived geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0; no open municipal-document licence is asserted. All outputs remain unsuitable for automatic address assignment.
