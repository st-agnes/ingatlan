# Western planning units – round 2, 2026-11-15

This packet adds **13 estimated search polygons** and proposes **one separately documented alias** across Keszthely, Dombóvár and Tata. It targets the 24 missing names assigned for this round, including the old unlocated planning-quarter names. Every new geometry is explicitly low confidence and estimated. No circles, Voronoi cells or parent-name aliases were created.

- **Keszthely: six names.** Történelmi belváros and Szalasztó follow the complete documentary boundary-road circuits, reconstructed from real OSM centrelines. The other four quarters use their complete municipal contained-street inventories to estimate whole occupied-quarter extents. These are quarter-scale envelopes, not building footprints. L2 retains a residential crescent by excluding the separately named centre, castle park and northern industrial estimates. Open rural edges, administrative limits and the Balaton shoreline remain coarse. Exact source unit codes VK1/VK3/L2/L3/Gi2/Ba1 and individual limitations accompany the features. The municipality explicitly names these quarters in its [local action plan](https://onkormanyzat.keszthely.hu/wp-content/uploads/document_library/12059/01_6.melleklet-_helyi_cselekvesi_terv-melleklet.pdf), printed pages 3–5; archived indexed primary-source text is in `keszthely/web-source-evidence.json`.
- **Tata: five names.** Külterületek is recovered from native drawing 9 of the archived 2022 statutory quarter map, using the existing documented PDF-to-EOV registration. It is the actual countryside MultiPolygon with the urban-quarter exclusions, not a subtraction invented from today's registry. The first nine native drawings had previously been exported, leaving this tenth filled path uncollected. Fácánoskert-lakópark, Levendulakert and Boglárka apartmanpark use explicit local estate-street envelopes, supported by municipal street and ITS documentation. Remeteség is explicitly `partialFootprint: true`: it estimates the connected residential quarter, with its broader forest/golf landscape unresolved.
- **Dombóvár: two names.** Egyéb, belvárost övező városi területek follows the municipal/KSH outer road circuit with the Belváros inner circuit excluded. Current-road continuations are inferred where historical textual roads do not close directly; actual network paths and off-named lengths are recorded in `dombovar/circuit-proof.json`. Népköztársaság útja is matched through actual OSM `old_name` tags. Perekac uses the original whole farmyard compound containing the named locality. It is explicitly `partialFootprint: true`, not a claim that the compound is the entire rural locality.

`alias-proposals.json` contains **Gunaras → Gunarasfürdő**. The primary [municipal name-use page](https://dombovar.hu/lakohelyunk/a-varosnev-hasznalata/) explicitly uses “Gunarasfürdő/Gunaras” and states that the same 2016 decree regulates both names for the same településrész. This is a verified identity, not an inference from a broad area containing a child. No duplicate Gunaras polygon is supplied. The evidence is archived separately.

`summary.json` and `unresolved.json` preserve the ten remaining assigned names. In particular, Dombóvár Külterületek means a group of built-up rural clusters in the source, not an arbitrary municipality remainder. The recorded Szilfás/Kiskonda/Nagykonda/Szarvasd labels are retained for subsequent map tracing; incomplete farmyard coverage was not presented as the full unit. Szuhay-domb was not blindly aliased to the existing Szuhajdomb footprint without its full source map. Körmend source leads are recorded but no point-only rings were generated.

## Reproduce offline

From the repository root:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=/workspace/ingatlan/.local/geodata python data/neighborhoods/expansion-20261115-round2/west-planning/rebuild.py
```

This re-extracts the native PDF path, derives the estate and documentary-road estimates, and rebuilds `features.geojson`. It needs Python, PyMuPDF, shapely and pyproj. The normal rebuild uses only the archived inputs and does not need network or the country PBF. `build.py` alone regenerates the final collection from the frozen city inputs. The initial Perekac source surface is archived without coordinate changes in `dombovar/source-rural-areas.geojson` and `partial-rural-features.geojson`.

Optional extraction scripts reproduce the source road/area and rural-label archives from the already-local Hungary PBF and additionally need pyosmium. Source snapshot 2026-11-12 has SHA256 `224897876ea131bcf0cb420e789640402d63aabe5e1491785aa4fca813aa641a`. Original way/node IDs, references, tags, versions and timestamps are retained. OSM-derived geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Public municipal sources are documentary evidence; no open municipal-map licence is asserted.

The native Tata registration gives 99.56% overlap with the current OSM municipality; small edge differences are preserved as source-map uncertainty. All other 12 features are fully contained. All 13 geometries are valid; no identical output footprints are assigned to different names. These estimates are unsuitable for automatic address assignment or cadastral decisions. Only this owned packet changed; registry integration, source-identity deduplication and publishing remain with the coordinating agent.
