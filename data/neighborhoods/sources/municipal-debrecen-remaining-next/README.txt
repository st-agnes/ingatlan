Debrecen — 23 additional ESTIMATED named-quarter candidates

This separate packet proposes 23 fine named quarter candidates, all outside the existing 26 primary Debrecen identities and earlier 6 estimated candidates. It makes no nationwide or municipal completeness claim. The candidate registry entry is registry-entry.json; these scripts write only within this packet.

Selected: Pallag, Epreskert, Nyulas, Úrrétje, Tócóliget, Akadémiakert, Kertváros, Köntöskert, Nagysándortelep, Hatvan utcai kert, Kismacs, Tócóvölgy, Sóház, Kondoros, Júliatelep, Boldogfalvikert, Tégláskert, Majorság, Nagymacs, Nagycsere, Haláp, Bánk, Ondód.

All 23 names appear exactly in the fine historical-quarter column of Table 79 in the municipal November 2023 FVS. All 23 have actual current same-city PBF place=suburb nodes inside the source polygons. The whole source polygons are valid, closed and within the same-PBF Debrecen municipality. Seven outlying built-up quarters are inside Debrecen, rather than separate municipality records. Historical names and current source points corroborate neighborhood identity only. Present whole-quarter extent, official/legal status and absolute ground accuracy are unverified.

Coordinates copy every published service EPSG:4326 vertex exactly. Original native EPSG:3857 query bytes and the complete published 77-feature GeoJSON are archived. Projection equivalence checks do not prove ground accuracy. The earlier independent 77 vs 26 review found a consistent source-minus-baseline west/south difference (median −61.063 m E, −27.218 m N across 25 same-name pairs); its cause and the more accurate source are unknown. No shift, fit, correction, snapping, repair, clipping, simplification, new closing edge or other geometry change is applied. Source lineage, reference period and reuse rights remain unestablished. Public access is not a licence. All candidates are nonofficial, nonhard, ESTIMATED and unsuitable for address assignment.

The source set mixes land-use types. This cohort uses garden/housing or distinct outlying built-up quarters with exact independent fine-name evidence; industrial/site/forest areas, spelling variants and point/extent conflicts remain deferred. Their exclusion is not a claim that all such land-use types lack genuine quarters. Józsa is a broader parent and is not split or cloned. FID 11 remains the source-mislabeled Széchenyikert duplicate overlapping Újkert; its literal name and geometry remain preserved in raw responses, never corrected, aliased, merged or admitted. all77-dispositions.json explains every source record.

Minor source disagreements are retained. Nyulas/Úrrétje intersect by about 979 m² (0.18% of the smaller area). Selected/baseline overlaps include Sóház/Kandia, Kondoros/Biharikert, Júliatelep/Sámsonikert and small boundary strips; exact metrics for every 23 × 32 comparison are retained. No selected identity duplicates a prior exact name or equal geometry. No selected polygon overlaps the previous six by 1 m² or more. These are estimate disagreements, rather than demonstrated legal common boundaries. Tócóvölgy specifically has an unresolved current extent because FVS 2023 discusses Lóskúti's separation from parts of it; no source reference date is inferred.

Only the selected original municipal raster map, factual label crops and paraphrased source facts are retained; the full narrative PDF stays ignored. FVS 2023 refers to 75 historical quarters designated by an already repealed 2007 ordinance and uses another, aggregated planning schema. It does not establish ArcGIS lineage or certify its present extent. OSM identity/reference evidence remains ODbL-1.0; unrestricted ArcGIS/map reuse rights are unestablished.
