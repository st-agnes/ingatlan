Eger2016 source semantics: qualified revision proposal, no global change

The proposal reclassifies23 of the existing32 source geometries as unreviewed historical source-defined quarter candidates and retains9 as planning units. It acquires zero new polygons and does not change any source geometry, name, ID or source plan identifier. It does not update the global registry, exports, documentation or counts. The proposed registry entry retains the existing dataset ID and must replace that entry rather than append duplicate records.

A proposed fine candidate means the complete NATIVE historical quarter-base-plan footprint as depicted in the2016-08 overview. It does not mean a demonstrated whole CURRENT/common-name quarter, a current ITS quarter, a current legal/cadastral boundary, or an address-assignment polygon. Older purple base-plan years remain explicit: Hajdúhegy2001, SzentMiklós2000, Belváros2001, Lajosváros-nyugat2003 and Galagonyás-dűlő2004. Green base units retain their2016 schema. A title saying a plan is for a quarter does not, by itself, establish ordinary/common-name extent.

This meaning is consistent with the current primary research layer's acceptance of historical Tata/Tatabánya statutory quarter candidates and historical Esztergom quarters with qualified dates, unknown current extents and unverified absolute accuracy. Eger additionally needs individual scope review because its2016 overview mixes four source schemas and includes groups, sites and partial areas. Planning purpose alone is not an exclusion, and industrial/rural function alone is not an exclusion.

The23 proposed historical candidates are Hajdúhegy, SzentMiklós, Belváros, Lajosváros–nyugat, Galagonyás-dűlő, Felnémet, Nagylapos, Rác hóstya, Hajdúhegy észak, Felsőváros, Károlyváros, Szépasszonyvölgy, Hatvani hóstya, K2 jelű út és környéke, Déli iparterület, Lajosváros kelet, Délkeleti külterület, Csákó, Tihamér, Maklári hóstya, Sánc, Belváros kelet and Vécseyvölgy. Each retains its exact original spelling in the actual GeoJSON. The audit gives a separate native base-versus-amendment and scope decision for every record.

The9 retained planning units are the three combined groups (Északkeleti külterület és Bikalegelő, Almagyar–Merengő, Cifra hóstya–Tetemvár), OMYA quarry/company site, Almár partial block, Belváros–Dobó tér subarea, former camping land with unresolved quarter-versus-Rác-hóstya-subarea identity, M1.23 northwest operational-site subarea, and residual general TSZT coverage. Child quarters are never cloned from a group polygon.

Three decisions differ from the earlier audit's assumptions. Native path164829 is the large closed2004 Galagonyás-dűlő base footprint; the separately dashed2012 cellar amendment lies inside it. The earlier partial-area reason cited that amendment as if it defined the selected base path. The official register describes the2004 base as a quarter plan; the proposal preserves this historical source footprint while leaving its present/common extent unknown. Conversely, the contemporaneous2016 law expressly narrows M1.23 to an operational area by road24; a2016 supporting report confirms the animal-shelter/pound project and that wider northwest land was unregulated. Former camping land has conflicting quarter language, but2016 HÉSZ§83 locates it within Rác hóstya and a2015 indexed study records its attachment there. That conflict remains unresolved and is not promoted.
