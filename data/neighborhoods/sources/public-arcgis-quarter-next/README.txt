Frozen candidates: Ispotály, Tócóskert, Kerekestelep, Homokkert, Lencztelep and Vargakert. The single curated registry entry is registry-entry.json. These are ESTIMATED source-placement/current-extent candidates with candidateBoundaryKind=neighbourhood, separate from primary polygons. Whole ordinary quarter extents, absolute ground accuracy, original lineage, boundary reference period and reuse rights remain unverified. All are nonofficial, nonhard and unsuitable for address assignment.

The six geometries copy the published service GeoJSON coordinates exactly. “Estimated” describes unresolved source placement/extent; we did not estimate new coordinates, shift, correct, snap, simplify, clip or repair them. Original native EPSG:3857 rings and the matching service EPSG:4326 response are both preserved. Independent projection checks show numerical agreement; this does not establish ground accuracy. Twenty-five same-name comparisons disagree systematically with the unreviewed OSM baseline (median source centroid 61.063 m west and 27.218 m south). No correction was fitted or applied.
